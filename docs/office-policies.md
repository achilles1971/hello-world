# Office Policies

## Office Hours and Access
The office is open 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday. Agents receive a door code after onboarding; codes are changed every quarter.

## Time Off and Coverage
Agents are independent contractors and do not accrue PTO. If you will be unavailable for more than 2 days, notify the Managing Broker and name a covering agent for your active clients in the CRM.

## Leaving the Brokerage
Give written notice to the Managing Broker. Pending transactions stay with the brokerage, and commission on them is paid under this policy once they close. Listings belong to the brokerage, not the individual agent.

## Complaints and Escalation
Client complaints must be forwarded to the Managing Broker within 1 business day. Do not respond to a legal threat or a regulatory inquiry yourself.
