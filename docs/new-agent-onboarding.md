# New Agent Onboarding Checklist

## Before Day One
- Sign the Independent Contractor Agreement and return it to the Operations Manager.
- Transfer your license to the brokerage through the state licensing portal. You may not list or show property under the brokerage until the transfer is approved.
- Provide proof of E&O insurance enrollment or opt into the brokerage group policy.
- Submit a W-9 so commission can be paid.

## First Week
1. Day 1: Meet with the Operations Manager for a systems walkthrough (CRM, transaction management platform, shared drive).
2. Day 1: IT sets up your company email account and password manager invitation. Change the temporary password within 24 hours.
3. Day 2: Complete MLS orientation and request your lockbox key access.
4. Day 3: Shadow a senior agent on at least one listing appointment or showing.
5. Day 5: Review the Transaction File Requirements SOP with the Transaction Coordinator.

## First 30 Days
- Complete the brokerage's fair housing training module and upload the certificate to your agent file.
- Order business cards and yard sign riders through the Marketing Coordinator. Brokerage branding must appear on all advertising.
- Schedule a 30-day check-in with the Managing Broker.

## Equipment and Access
Agents use their own laptop and phone. The brokerage provides email, CRM, transaction platform, and e-signature licenses. Lost or compromised passwords must be reported to IT the same day.
