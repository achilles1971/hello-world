# Commission and Expense Policy

## Commission Splits
New agents start on a 70/30 split (agent/brokerage). After $3,000,000 in closed volume in a calendar year, the split moves to 80/20 for the rest of that year. The split resets every January 1.

## Commission Disbursement
Commission is paid by direct deposit within 2 business days after the title company's funds clear and the transaction file is marked complete. Commission cannot be advanced before closing.

## Expense Reimbursement
1. Submit the receipt through the expense form on the shared drive within 30 days of the purchase.
2. Include the property address or marketing campaign the expense relates to.
3. Only expenses pre-approved by the Office Manager are reimbursed. Mileage is not reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next commission disbursement or on the 15th of the month, whichever comes first.

## Brokerage Fees
A transaction fee of $395 is charged to the agent on every closed transaction. There is no monthly desk fee.
