# Transaction File Requirements

## Submitting Contracts
All fully executed purchase agreements must be uploaded to the transaction management platform within 24 hours of the final signature. Late uploads are reported to the Managing Broker.

Every file must include the agency disclosure, the seller's property disclosure (or exemption), the lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978, and the earnest money receipt.

## Earnest Money
Earnest money must be delivered to the escrow holder within 3 business days of contract acceptance, unless the contract states a different deadline. Agents may never hold earnest money personally.

## New Listings
1. Upload the signed listing agreement and seller disclosure before the listing goes live.
2. The Transaction Coordinator checks the MLS entry for accuracy within 1 business day.
3. Photos must be professional; phone photos are not permitted for MLS listings.

## Offers
Present all written offers to the seller as soon as practical, including offers received after another offer has been accepted, unless the seller has waived this in writing.

## Closing
Upload the final settlement statement within 2 business days of closing. Commission is not disbursed until the file is marked complete by the Transaction Coordinator.
